Workshops & retreats

Make time for the conversations you’ve been putting off. Vivian’s guided workshops and couples’ retreats give you a chance to practise what to say and decide what comes next.

Practise what to say

In these group workshops, Vivian draws on Unspoken Costs to help participants explore the beliefs and emotions that shape their money decisions. Guided reflection and conversation exercises give people a chance to try opening a difficult conversation and hear how others approach it.

  • Identify a topic you’ve been putting off and what makes it hard to raise.
  • Try language for bringing it up clearly and respectfully.
  • Choose a next step you can take after the workshop.

Couples’ retreats

Step away from everyday demands for a guided weekend focused on money, trust, and the decisions you want to make together. Vivian uses paired exercises, group discussion, and private reflection to help couples start conversations they can continue at home.

What you’ll work on together

  • Talk about what money means to each of you.
  • Clarify a shared goal or a decision you’ve been avoiding.
  • Agree how to keep the conversation going at home.

Retreats run periodically, depending on interest and group availability.
