Couples’ retreats

Take a weekend away from everyday demands to talk about money, trust, and the decisions you want to make together. Vivian guides you through conversations that can be hard to start at home.

Ask about upcoming retreats

What you’ll do together

The weekend combines time to reflect, exercises with your partner, and guided group discussion. It’s for couples who want to make space for conversations they’ve been postponing.

Explore what money means

Reflect on the experiences and assumptions you each bring to conversations about spending, saving, and sharing decisions.

Raise a difficult topic

Practise opening a conversation with your partner and hearing what matters to each of you.

Plan the next conversation

Choose a shared goal or decision to revisit and agree how you’ll follow up at home.
