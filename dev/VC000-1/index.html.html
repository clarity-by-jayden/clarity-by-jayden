The book

Unspoken Costs

How to Have Relationship-Saving Money Conversations

Everyone hates hard conversations, mostly because no one hands us the tools to make them easier. Unspoken Costs is your guide to relationship-saving money conversations: what to say, what not to say, and how to begin when your heart is pounding and the subject feels too big. Inside, you will find real scripts, honest stories, and practical language you can use right away. You will learn to increase trust, open the lines of communication, and talk about anything, at any time, with the people who matter most. Every page comes from Vivian Cobb’s lived experience of what silence costs.

Workshops & retreats

Practice what to say.

In workshops, groups use guided reflection and conversation exercises to put ideas from Unspoken Costs into practice. Participants get a chance to try opening a difficult conversation and plan how to follow through.

Vivian also offers periodic couples’ retreats for partners who want to talk about money, expectations, and shared goals in a facilitated setting.

Meet your speaker

Vivian Cobb

After her husband became seriously ill, Vivian discovered a financial crisis they had never talked about. Rebuilding her life changed how she understood silence, trust, and responsibility.

Today she brings personal storytelling and practical conversation tools to stages and workshops, helping people find words for topics that are hard to raise at home and at work.
