A

Website & Messaging

Structure, positioning, course journeys and conversion

EssentialRecommended start

I recommend restructuring the website so it is much clearer for each type of visitor and easier for people to take the right next step. I’d also advise moving away from Wix. The 10-week course needs its own dedicated landing page, while one-day and one-to-one training for experienced barbers should sit in a clearly separate area. I’d also replace the catch-all contact form with purpose-specific pages and forms — for example, a dedicated models page and model form — so each enquiry route can ask the right questions and feed into the right list.

£1,200Lead time: 3–5 weeks

C

Email Campaign Strategy

Audience capture, nurture, testing and conversion

Essential

Email is currently massively underutilised. We don’t yet know how strongly your audience will engage with it, but there is also no data suggesting they won’t. I recommend building a proper email campaign strategy so you can capture people who are interested but not ready to enrol immediately, keep the academy front of mind, and test which messages generate the strongest response. I’d separate prospective learners from existing barbers so each audience receives relevant messaging and offers.

£500Lead time: 2–3 weeks

E

Content Strategy

Year-round awareness, nurture, trust and conversion

Recommended

You’re already producing promising video content, particularly on Instagram. I recommend putting a clearer strategy around it so each piece of content has a job. I’d structure the system around Reach, Confidence, Decision and Relationship, giving you a year-round content approach that nurtures people before they enter a cohort launch window and supports the wider recruitment system rather than sitting separately from it.

£500Lead time: 2–3 weeks

G

Learner Onboarding

Pre-course learner experience and confidence

Recommended

The WhatsApp groups already give learners a personal point of contact before they start, and I’d keep that. I recommend strengthening the experience around it with a simple digital onboarding area where learners can access everything they need before day one — including parking, timings, what to bring, kit information, FAQs, expectations, tutor introductions and useful academy information.

£300Lead time: 2–3 weeks
block_i×
I

Existing Barber / One-Day Training Growth

Secondary revenue stream and professional barber development

Growth opportunity

I recommend giving your one-day and one-to-one training for experienced barbers a much clearer identity. I’d position it as a separate part of the academy, with its own website area, forms, mailing list and messaging. That gives you a stronger way to market specialist training, build a separate audience of working barbers and generate additional revenue between main cohort launches without confusing people who are completely new to barbering.

£500Lead time: 2–3 weeks
block_b×
B

Lead Capture & Enquiry-to-Enrolment

Attribution, follow-up, conversion and webinar lead generation

EssentialRecommended start

I recommend creating one joined-up process from the moment somebody makes contact through to enrolment. That means clearer source tracking, tailored forms, a consistent “How did you hear about us?” question, simple funnel stages, and a defined follow-up journey through phone call, academy tour, application and enrolment. Your personal phone call should stay central. I’d also introduce a monthly open webinar as a low-pressure lead magnet: outside launch periods it can build the mailing list or waitlist, and during launch periods it can direct people straight into the course journey.

£500Lead time: 2–3 weeks
block_d×
D

Reusable 12-Week Cohort Launch Campaign

Revenue, cohort recruitment and repeatable campaign structure

EssentialRecommended start

I recommend creating a reusable 12-week launch campaign that becomes the standard recruitment process for every future cohort. The academy should nurture potential learners all year through content, email, webinars and learner stories. Then, 12 weeks before each cohort, that audience moves into a structured campaign covering awareness, course education, testimonials, webinars, academy tours, email, social content, direct follow-up, urgency and final enrolment activity. I’d also make the £2,000 rate a genuine early-bird offer within this campaign before returning to the standard £2,300 price.

£900Lead time: 3–4 weeks
block_f×
F

Learner Case Studies & Social Proof

Testimonials, proof and real learner outcomes

Recommended

I recommend making learner stories a much more deliberate part of your marketing. Rather than relying on occasional testimonials, I’d create a repeatable process that follows learners from the start of their journey through graduation and into employment, self-employment or business ownership. That gives prospective learners stronger evidence of the full transformation and creates proof that can be reused across the website, email, social media and future cohort launches.

£350Lead time: 2–3 weeks
block_h×
H

Alumni & Graduate Community

Referrals, proof and long-term relationships

Growth opportunity

I think there is a missed opportunity once learners graduate. I recommend creating a simple alumni system so you can stay in touch, follow their progress, capture future case studies, ask for referrals and bring graduates back into the academy ecosystem where relevant. Over time, each cohort should help support the credibility and recruitment of the next one.

£300Lead time: 2–3 weeks
