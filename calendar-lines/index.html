A simple way to scan your day

Your calendars, laid out as lines.

1

Connect your Google account

Sign in with Google and grant Calendar Lines read-only access to the calendars you choose.

2

Choose your lines

Show or hide calendars, then look ahead across 7, 14 or 30 days.

3

See what’s next

The platform-style panel shows the next timed event. All-day events stay in the timetable as standalone stops.

Read-only by design. Calendar Lines reads calendar names and event details to display your timetable. It does not create, change, share or delete events.

Each person authorizes their own Google account. Your calendar data is returned to your browser to display the timetable. Read the Calendar Lines privacy policy for details.

Calendar Lines is an independent tool by Clarity by Jayden. It is not affiliated with Google.
