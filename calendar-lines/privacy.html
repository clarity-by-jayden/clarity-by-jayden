Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Calendar Lines is a read-only timetable for Google Calendar, operated by Jayden Cooke as Clarity by Jayden. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it handles that information.

What Calendar Lines accesses

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly to list the calendars available to your Google account, including their names, identifiers and display colours.
  • calendar.events.readonly to read event details from calendars you select, so the app can show your timetable. This can include event titles, dates and times, locations, calendar names and links to the events in Google Calendar.

The app does not request your Google password. Google handles sign-in and authorization.

How your information is used

Calendar information is used only to build and display your timetable, including the next timed event and the dated schedule. Calendar Lines does not create, edit, share or delete calendar events. It does not sell calendar information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles.

The app runs as the Google user who opened it. Calendar information is retrieved through Google Apps Script and Google Calendar, then returned to that user’s browser for display. The app’s code does not send event details to Jayden or store event details in a separate database.

What is saved in your browser

Calendar Lines saves your chosen calendar-line display preferences in local browser storage on that device, so your line choices can be remembered. It uses a short hash of your primary calendar identifier as the preference key. The app does not save event details in that browser storage. You can remove these preferences by clearing site data for the Calendar Lines app in your browser.

Service providers and processing

Google Apps Script and Google Calendar provide the hosting, sign-in and calendar services needed to run Calendar Lines. Google may process technical information under its own terms and privacy policies. Calendar Lines does not provide your calendar contents to other service providers.

Google’s API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, applies to the app’s use of Google user data: Google API Services User Data Policy.

Retention and revoking access

Calendar Lines does not keep a separate copy of your event details after the request is used to display the timetable. Your browser-stored line preferences remain until you clear them. You can revoke Calendar Lines’ Google access at any time from your Google Account connections and permissions.

Security

Google’s authorization is used to access only the read-only calendar permissions listed above. Do not use Calendar Lines on a device or account you do not trust. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Calendar Lines’ features or data handling change. The latest version will be published at this address, with the updated date shown at the top.
